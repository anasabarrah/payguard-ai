Outdoor gear, paid with PayPal

A small demo store that sells through PayPal. Every order is created and captured on the server with the Orders v2 API, then scored by PayGuard AI in the seller's dashboard.

__JUDGE_BOX__

To pay, click a PayPal button and log in with a sandbox personal (buyer) account from developer.paypal.com → Testing tools → Sandbox accounts. The payment then shows up in the PayGuard dashboard within a few seconds, with its risk score.
